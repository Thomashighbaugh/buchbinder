# Faction Template

Use this structure for a `## [Faction Name]` section in `./book/knowledge/factions.md`.

```markdown
## {Faction Name}

- **Type:** {family | guild | government | military | religion | company | community | criminal | other}
- **Status:** {active | hidden | declining | defeated | disbanded | unknown}
- **Members:** {character names}
- **Locations:** {location names}
- **Related:** {other factions, systems}

### Purpose
What this faction wants and why it exists.

### Power Base
Resources, influence, territory, legitimacy, secrets, rituals, or leverage.

### Members
Important members and their roles.

### Conflicts
Internal divisions and external opposition.
```

Verify named members exist in `character-profiles.md` and cross-reference back. Record later changes to a faction as **Progressions**, not by editing the opening values.
