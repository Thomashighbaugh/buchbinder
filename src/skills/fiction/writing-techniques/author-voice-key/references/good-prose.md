# Good English Prose — Qualities to Write Toward

The qualities below separate strong prose from output that is merely correct. They are the **positive counterpart** to [`../bot-slop.md`](../bot-slop.md): that guide catalogs what to remove, this one names what to build. The list is reproduced as given.

**Where this is used**

- **The model's output** — compressed into the always-on `@Prose Craft` directive in the preset ([Presets and Prompts](../presets-guide.md)), so every reply is written toward them.
- **Greetings** — the greeting is the most-copied text on the card, so it is written and scored against them ([First Message and Alternate Greetings](../character-card/greetings.md)).
- **Verification** — prose quality is a scored dimension ([Verification](../character-card/verification.md) §1.2, dimension 11).

## The qualities

1. **Control of voice** — the narration has a consistent, identifiable sensibility, and that voice is doing work beyond decoration: it carries judgment, mood, and meaning the way sound carries a song.
2. **Sentences with varied cadence** — rhythm alternates between long and short, latinate and plain, so paragraphs breathe. Monotone sentence length is the most common tell of weak prose no matter how correct the grammar.
3. **Precision of word choice** — the right noun and verb instead of an adverb patching up a lazy one. The revision instinct is to cut modifiers, not add them.
4. **Concreteness over abstraction** — emotional states are rendered as physical, sensory, situational fact rather than named. "Grief" is a cold cup of tea she doesn't drink, not "she felt profound grief."
5. **The five senses deployed unevenly and pointedly** — smell and touch often neglected by amateurs; the best work uses them precisely and sparingly, so a sensory detail registers as information rather than texture for its own sake.
6. **Subtext** — characters say approximately what they mean, and the gap between their words and their situation is where the scene lives. Dialogue carries the load of what is left unsaid.
7. **Dialogue that is distinct per speaker** — you could remove the attribution tags and still know who is talking, because vocabulary, syntax, rhythm, and evasions are individuated.
8. **Resistance to exposition** — backstory arrives through objects, actions, textures, and misdirected remarks; the dump is displaced or dissolved. Information is rationed and earned.
9. **Structural architecture** — the plot is load-bearing: every scene changes the situation, and the arrangement of chapters (chronology, framing, braiding, withheld knowledge) is itself meaningful.
10. **Momentum and causality** — events follow from character rather than from authorial convenience; coincidence may open a door but never solve the problem.
11. **A living, specific world** — place is rendered with local particularity: weather, economy, speech, food, labor, hierarchy. The setting could not be swapped into another novel without loss.
12. **Characters with interior contradiction** — people want incompatible things, act against their stated values, and are capable of surprising the reader without becoming implausible.
13. **Moral seriousness** — the novel engages stakes larger than personal satisfaction, and the author's intelligence shows in how tensions are held open rather than resolved glibly.
14. **Themes enacted, not announced** — the meaning emerges from the arrangement of events and images, not from a character stepping forward to state the point. No thesis sentences doing the theme's work.
15. **Imagery that recurs and mutates** — a motif (a scar, a river, a debt) returns changed at each appearance, accumulating meaning across the whole book.
16. **Inevitability and surprise together** — the ending feels both unexpected and unsurpassable, the way a good joke's punchline is. Retrospectively, it was always the only ending.
17. **Compression and restraint** — the best passages omit whatever can be inferred; the reader is trusted to assemble meaning, and the omitted thing is often the emotional center.
18. **Tonal coherence** — even a book spanning comedy and horror has a governing tone that unifies shifts rather than whipsawing the reader arbitrarily.
19. **Show-then-trust pacing** — the writer establishes a pattern, then violates it meaningfully; setups are paid off (or deliberately refused) with awareness of the expectation created.
20. **Revision visible as polish** — paragraphs are irreducible: try to cut one sentence and something is lost. This is the fingerprint of many drafts.
21. **Physical and psychological accuracy** — biology, craft, labor, and psychology behave as they actually do, which is what lets the reader suspend disbelief elsewhere.
22. **Point of view used as a tool** — the chosen lens (first, close third, omniscient, unreliable) is exploited for what only it can do, not merely defaulted to.
23. **Tension sustained at the level of the sentence** — even quiet domestic scenes hold pressure because the reader knows something is at stake or feels something is being withheld.
24. **Restrained, non-adjectival emotion** — the cataclysmic moments are frequently the calmest in register, which produces more feeling than escalation would.
25. **Immersive interiority, but not solipsism** — access to a character's mind deepens rather than narrows the world; their consciousness is a window, not a cell.
26. **Avoidance of stock constructions** — clichés, reflexive similes, sententious summary, and filler are ruthlessly excised, because the writer hears where the reader's eye will glide past.
27. **Narrative generosity** — the book gives the reader something to feel, think, and argue about; it does not merely perform its own cleverness.
28. **Originality of angle** — even a familiar story is told from an unexpected aperture: a perspective, a form, a structure, a moral premise not seen before in that combination.
29. **The ending earns its weight** — resolution is proportionate to the promises made, and ambiguity, when used, is controlled rather than evasive.
30. **A felt sense of obsession** — the writer was clearly trying to solve something that mattered to them, and that pressure is legible on every page.

## Working with the list

The qualities are a direction, not a checklist to recite. Two notes on applying them:

- **They are the positive form of the slop rules.** Where [`bot-slop.md`](../bot-slop.md) names a construction to cut, a quality here names the habit to build in its place. Concrete-over-abstraction (4) is the fix for "she felt profound grief"; avoidance of stock constructions (26) is the fix for the catalogued clichés; restrained emotion (24) is the fix for melodrama.
- **A scene is judged on the whole.** No single quality is a pass/fail gate on its own. The test is whether the passage would lose something if a sentence were cut (20), whether the voice could belong to another card (1, 7), and whether the reader is trusted to assemble meaning (17).

## Alternative Thoughts

- **The list as a generation prompt.** Feeding all thirty qualities verbatim to the model each turn produces purple prose and self-consciousness; the preset compresses them into a short directive instead. The full list is for the human writing and reviewing, not for pasting.
- **Treating them as a rubric.** Scoring prose against thirty dimensions is slow and noisy. The guides use a small number of scored dimensions (register, prose quality) and hold the rest as direction.
